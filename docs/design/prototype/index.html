Don't Burn Your Brain: the drawn screens

Every screen of the app as it was drawn for the spec. The compact panel, Jira review and the Workspaces settings are live. How each screen behaves is written down in docs/spec/design.md.

Menu bar and compact panel

One click on the menu bar item opens this panel. Both panels are live: try Pause, Break, +15 and Switch, or start a block on the right.

One number. Time left is the largest thing in the panel. Seconds are small on purpose.

The block as a strip. Pale gaps are the breaks, so you see where you are in the rhythm and when the next break comes.

Four actions in fixed places. Pause, break, add time, switch workspace.

Only what needs you. The amber strip appears when an agent waits. Working and idle sessions stay one step away.

The menu bar item in its five states

Day off. Out of office leaves nothing planned for today, or you took the day off. Untracked time is not counted.

Expanded panel: wide on the Mac, pages on the phone

Option B, on the iPhone: pages inside a small panel

A bar at the bottom switches between the pages. Drawn here at Mac size so the two can be compared. On the Mac this one is not built.

A fits a big screen. One key and you see every session, the plan for today and the totals together.

B never grows. It behaves the same on a laptop and has the same structure as the iPhone app.

Decided: A on the Mac. The Mac has room for an overview, and the phone uses B anyway.

Quick functions

Three ways to act without opening the panel at all.

Open the command bar⌥ Space
Switch between sessions⌥ Tab
Pause, break, focus the waiting session⌥ SpacethenPBF
Start preset 1 to 3⌥ Spacethen123
Expand or collapse the open panel⌘ E
Jump to a session while the panel is open⌘ 1to⌘ 9

Only ⌥ Space and ⌥ Tab are global. Both can be changed in Settings.

Two numbers per task. Tracked is what the timer measured, breaks included when the workspace counts them. The other is what Jira gets: rounded by the workspace rule first, then yours to change with − and + or by typing a value.

Changes after sending. Change the hours and the app updates that worklog in Jira. Removing a row deletes the worklog there.

Nothing leaves without you. Tasks without a Jira issue stay out, and nothing is sent until you press the button.

Out of office: a day you are not working

An Out of office entry in a calendar is not a meeting. It says you are not working, so the app marks the day and stays quiet.

In the menu bar and the panel

Out of office belongs to the workspace whose calendar it is in. If that leaves nothing planned for today, the whole day is off.

In the day plan

The Friday from the left panel. Globex is out, and the Acme and Personal blocks stay where you put them.

Never a meeting, never time worked. Out of office is not logged anywhere. If it covers only part of a day, only those hours are off.

Quiet for that workspace. Its meetings are hatched, not counted and not announced, and its planned blocks are not suggested.

The app only asks. Start a block for a workspace that is out and you confirm once. You can also take a day off yourself from the command bar, with no calendar involved.

History keeps the mark. The day shows as out of office, so a short week does not look like forgotten time.

Workspaces are your own data

Acme, Globex and Personal are only sample data in these drawings. You create as many workspaces as you need in Settings, opened with ⌘ and comma. This window is live too: rename a workspace, change its color, add one or remove one.

Folders tie sessions to a workspace. A Claude or Codex session started in one of these folders shows up under that workspace.

Jira and calendar are per workspace. A workspace with no Jira, such as a personal one, never appears in Jira review. Out of office in a workspace's calendar marks that workspace as off.

Rounding and breaks are set here. One employer may want quarter hours with breaks counted, another exact time without them. Breaks count by default.

Decided

  • Expanded panel: A on the Mac, B on the phone.
  • Quick actions: Pause, Break, +15 and Switch. End block lives in the right-click menu.
  • Shortcuts: ⌥ Space and ⌥ Tab. Both can be changed in Settings if they clash with something.

Sounds

  • Soft chime when a focus interval ends
  • A different chime when a break ends
  • Longer tone when the block ends
  • Short tick when an agent needs you, which can be muted during focus
